Lorsque le temps est za

This project emerged from an obsession with time, not as a linear progression but rather as an omnipresent force that can both corrupt and liberate. Lorsque le temps est za is a collection of approximations; assembled photographs, prose fragments, and philosophical musings that lead to meditations on temporal experience, identity, and the impossibility of holding onto fleeting moments. The photographs represent an attempt to freeze moments, moments that resist and exist beyond their capture. They capture time outside of time: a kiss, an intimate glance, a stranger’s perfume. These images pair with fragmented and contradictory text that refuse coherence, spiraling instead through repeated refrains. The refrain “lorsque le temps est za” (when time is za) threads throughout. “Za” is, in some ways, meaningless, acting as a placeholder for what cannot be articulated. This void highlights the central tension of the book: the impossibility of understanding time, even as it shapes everything. Central to this work is also being a black man navigating the world.. Time operates differently for racialized bodies, carrying the weight of stereotypes, ignorance, or simply racism. I explore some of these vulnerabilities through the recurrence of tears, not as a weakness but as an admission of beauty, sorrow, joy, and uncertainty. This project treats “futility” itself as a creative and critical method. In a world demanding constant productivity, I embrace this ‘uselessness’ as a form of “révolte”. It is a way to challenge the archival impulse, critique the stability of memory, and explore the spontaneous, contradictory, and often heavy nature of lived experience.
