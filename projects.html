[2024] Lorsque le temps est za

This project emerged from an obsession with time, not as a linear progression but rather as an omnipresent force that can both corrupt and liberate. Lorsque le temps est za is a collection of approximations; assembled photographs, prose fragments, and philosophical musings that lead to meditations on temporal experience, identity, and the impossibility of holding onto fleeting moments.

Moving Stillness: The Rythm of Bogota[2024]

This exhibit in Bogota was a study of the continuity and discontinuity of moving bodies and rigid buildings. It was a sensory experience, one where the immobile meets movement, where projection meets the physicality of texture.

[2024] DatSoundRadio

At DatSoundRadio, we explored the conversations between artists and the essence of their creative processes. The vulnerability of intimate interviews and the resonance of carefully selected songs. We captured, edited, and photographed moments of artistic revelation
