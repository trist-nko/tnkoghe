Moving Stillness: The Rythm of Bogotá

Each country has its own rhythm. Each city has its own ambiance. Movements are thus peculiar and idiosyncratic to situated contexts. This exhibition explored the movement of bodies and the shape of environments. The curves of hips and the edges of buildings. We captured, sculpted, and photographed limbs acting as bridges, smiles as lighthouses, skin as walls, windows as eyes, beams as legs, and doors as noses. There is beauty in contradictions. In this exhibition, we connected the stillness of buildings and architecture to the shifting and changing qualities of the bodies inhabiting those spaces. We juxtaposed them through images, videos, and sculptures to encapsulate the relationship between permanence and finitude, between rigidity and malleability, between the firm and the porous. Moving Stillness: The Rhythm of Bogota is a study of the continuity and discontinuity of moving bodies and rigid buildings. It is a sensory experience--one where the immobile meets movement, where projection meets the physicality of texture.
