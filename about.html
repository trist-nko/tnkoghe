About Me

I am a PhD Candidate in Social Sciences. I am connecting the theoretical and practical concerns of Sociology, Gender Studies, and Design and Computation Arts. My interests and expertise exist at the intersections of these subjects. I study and write about GenAI, masculinity, fashion, sexuality, and blackness.

When I am not busy wondering why French theorists have to be so verbose, or what "gap" I am inventing so that I can beg for more money, I am learning how to code and how to design websites. I am writing (not my dissertation, never my dissertation) and I am taking photos.
